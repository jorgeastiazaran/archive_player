Fuentes sugeridas como base de los archivos:


Dedicated Audio & Music Archives

• Free Music Archive (FMA): Founded by a legendary freeform radio station, this massive interactive library offers high-quality, legal audio downloads spanning 16 distinct musical genres under Creative Commons and public domain licenses.
• Musopen: A specialized non-profit educational charity on a mission to "set music free." It records and catalogs high-quality classical music tracks, sheet music, and music textbooks completely restriction-free.
• IMSLP (Petrucci Music Library): The ultimate, indispensable public domain sheet music repository, hosting hundreds of thousands of scores and historic audio recordings.
• Freesound: A massive collaborative database of Creative Commons-licensed audio samples, field recordings, and vintage television/movie sound effects.
• Open Music Archive: A UK-based initiative dedicated to digitizing out-of-copyright, historic 78rpm-era music recordings for free public use and remixing.


Dedicated Film & Video Archives

• The Public Domain Review (Films): A beautifully curated digital library that highlights out-of-copyright classic films, early animations, silent shorts, and vintage surrealist cinema.
• The Film Detective: A specialized streaming archive focused on preserving and showcasing restored versions of Old Hollywood features, silent films, and rare black-and-white cult classics.
• UbuWeb: The premier web repository for avant-garde, experimental, and independent film and video art, catering to alternative and historic moving images.
• Public Domain Movies: A straightforward, no-frills online catalog tracking full-length features whose copyrights have lapsed, heavily featuring classic horror, film noir, westerns, and early animation.
• National Film Board of Canada (NFB): An expansive digital screening room providing over 5,000 free documentaries, animations, and alternative dramas
